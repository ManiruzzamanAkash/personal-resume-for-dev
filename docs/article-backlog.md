# Article backlog

The daily writing task takes the **first unchecked item** whose topic was
not covered in the last 7 posts, writes it following
[`article-playbook.md`](./article-playbook.md), and ticks it (`[x]`) with
the published slug. When fewer than 5 items are left unchecked, it appends
5 new ideas at the bottom that fit the playbook's topic mix.

Edit freely: reorder to change what comes next, add your own ideas, or
delete ones you don't want. A line starting with `!` is a priority — it
jumps the queue.

Format: `- [ ] Working title — format — category — main reader`
